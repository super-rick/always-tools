How to read Morse code

Morse code uses dots and dashes to represent each letter or number. A dot is shown as a period, a dash as a hyphen, letters are separated by a space, and words are separated by a forward slash.

FAQ

What is Morse code?

Morse code is a method of encoding letters, numbers, and punctuation as sequences of short and long signals called dots and dashes.

How do I read the Morse output?

A dot is shown as a period and a dash as a hyphen. Letters are separated by a single space, and words are separated by a forward slash (/).
